OpenClaw: How to Set Up Your Own AI Agent

February 2026 · 14 min read

Imagine having an employee who works 24/7, never calls in sick, remembers every conversation you've ever had, and costs less than your monthly coffee habit. That's not a fantasy — it's exactly what an AI agent does. And with OpenClaw, you can set one up in about 30 minutes.

I've been running an AI agent for my side business, and it's already handling product creation, content writing, email monitoring, and research. In this guide, I'll show you exactly what OpenClaw is, why it's different from ChatGPT, and how to get started.

What Is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI agent framework that runs on your own computer. Think of it as the operating system for your personal AI assistant.

Unlike ChatGPT or Claude (which you use through a browser), OpenClaw runs in the background on your machine — continuously. It connects to your messaging apps, your files, your calendar, and your tools. You can text it on Telegram or WhatsApp just like messaging a friend, and it'll respond with the full context of everything you've discussed before.

The key differences from a regular chatbot:

  • Persistent memory. It remembers every conversation, decision, and preference. No more re-explaining your business every time you open a new chat.
  • Always on. It runs 24/7, checking your email, monitoring your shop, running scheduled tasks — even while you sleep.
  • Real tool access. It can browse the web, read and write files, run scripts, manage your calendar, and interact with real apps.
  • Messaging integration. Talk to it through Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, Signal, or iMessage — whatever you already use.
  • Customizable personality. You define how it thinks, speaks, and behaves. It becomes your assistant, not a generic one.

What You Need to Get Started

The barrier to entry is surprisingly low:

  • A computer — Mac, Windows, or Linux. Even a Raspberry Pi works for basic setups. It needs to stay on for the agent to run continuously.
  • Node.js — The runtime environment. Free, takes 2 minutes to install.
  • An AI API key — From Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI (ChatGPT). This is your agent's "brain." Costs vary, but expect $5-$30/month for moderate use.
  • A messaging app — Telegram is the easiest to set up. WhatsApp and Discord work too.
  • 30 minutes — That's genuinely all the setup time.

Quick Start: Your First 3 Steps

Here's a preview of how the setup works. (The complete, detailed walkthrough is in our premium guide below.)

Step 1: Install OpenClaw

Open your terminal and run:

npm install -g openclaw

That's it. One command. OpenClaw installs globally and is ready to configure.

Step 2: Initial Configuration

Run the setup wizard:

openclaw init

This walks you through connecting your AI provider (Anthropic or OpenAI), setting up your workspace, and configuring basic preferences. The wizard is interactive — it asks questions and sets everything up based on your answers.

Step 3: Connect Telegram

The easiest way to talk to your agent. You'll create a Telegram bot through BotFather (Telegram's official bot-creation tool), grab the token, and add it to your OpenClaw config. The whole process takes about 5 minutes, and the setup guide walks you through every click.

Once connected, you can message your agent just like texting a friend. Ask it questions, give it tasks, have it research something — all from your phone.

Real Use Cases

Here's how real people are using AI agents for their side hustles:

Managing an Etsy Shop

Your agent monitors your shop, tracks which products are getting views, suggests pricing adjustments, drafts responses to customer messages, and creates new product listings when you describe what you want. One seller told me they went from spending 2 hours a day on shop management to 15 minutes.

Content Creation Pipeline

Tell your agent your content calendar for the week. It drafts blog posts, creates social media captions, generates email newsletters, and even suggests hashtags and posting times. You review, tweak, and publish. What used to be a full day of work becomes a 45-minute review session.

Research and Analysis

Need to understand a new market? Your agent can search the web, analyze competitors, compile pricing data, and deliver a structured report. I had mine research the top planner sellers on Etsy — their pricing, bestsellers, tag strategies, and review patterns. The report was ready in 10 minutes.

Email and Communication

Your agent checks your email on a schedule, surfaces anything important, and drafts responses. It learns your communication style over time, so the drafts get better and better. You're never out of touch, but you're also never drowning in inbox noise.

Freelance Work Automation

If you freelance on Upwork or similar platforms, your agent can draft proposals, create portfolio pieces, research client industries before calls, and even track time and generate invoices. It's like having a business manager who works for pennies.

The Cost Reality

Let's be transparent about costs:

  • OpenClaw itself: Free (open source)
  • AI API usage: $5-$30/month for typical side-hustle use (depends on how much you use it)

Compare that to hiring a virtual assistant ($500-$2,000/month) or spending your own time on tasks the agent handles. The ROI is almost instant.
