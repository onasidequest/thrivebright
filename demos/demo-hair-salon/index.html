What we do

Whether you're here for a quick trim or a full transformation, every client gets our full attention.

Color & Balayage

Full color, highlights, balayage, and corrective color. We specialize in lived-in, low-maintenance looks that grow out beautifully.

Cuts & Styling

Precision cuts for all hair types. Bobs, layers, pixies, textured cuts — tailored to your face shape and lifestyle.

Blowouts & Special Occasions

Weddings, proms, date nights, or just because. Blowouts, updos, braids, and styling that lasts.

The Bloom experience

  • Consultation first, always

    Every appointment starts with a conversation. We listen to what you want, look at your hair, and make honest recommendations.

  • Products that are actually good

    We use and sell salon-quality, cruelty-free products. No drugstore substitutes — your hair deserves better.

  • Relaxed, never rushed

    We don't double-book. Your stylist is yours for the entire appointment. Grab a coffee, settle in.

  • Inclusive and welcoming

    All hair types, all textures, all people. Everyone is welcome at Bloom. Period.
