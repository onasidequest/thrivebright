What we do

We handle every part of your outdoor space — from the first sketch to the last mulch delivery.

Landscape Design & Planting

Custom designs built around your property, your climate, and how you actually use your yard. Native plants, perennials, and seasonal color.

Hardscaping & Patios

Patios, walkways, retaining walls, fire pits, and outdoor kitchens. Built to last through Carolina weather.

Seasonal Maintenance

Mulching, pruning, leaf cleanup, bed prep, and irrigation checks. We keep it looking its best year-round.

How we work

  • On-site consultation first

    We walk your property, listen to your vision, take measurements, and talk budget — before we draw a single line.

  • Design you can actually see

    Detailed plans with plant selections, materials, and layout. You approve everything before we break ground.

  • Built by our own crew

    No subcontractors on your property. Our team does the install — the same people who designed it.

  • We stand behind our work

    One-year plant guarantee. If something doesn't make it, we replace it. That's the deal.
