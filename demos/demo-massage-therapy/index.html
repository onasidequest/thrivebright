What we offer

Every session starts with a brief intake — where you hurt, how you live, what you need. The work is tailored to you, not a menu.

Deep Tissue Massage

Firm, targeted work on the deeper muscle layers — chronic tension, postural patterns, and stubborn knots. This one gets results.

Swedish Massage

Full-body relaxation with smooth, flowing strokes. Reduces cortisol, improves circulation, quiets the nervous system.

Sports Massage

Pre-event prep or post-event recovery — focused on performance, flexibility, and injury prevention.

How we work

  • We listen before we start

    Every session begins with a brief conversation — what's bothering you, what your week looks like, what you need today.

  • Licensed therapists only

    Every therapist holds an NC massage license and maintains continuing education in their specialty. No exceptions.

  • Pressure is always your call

    We work at the level that serves you — and we check in throughout. Deeper isn't always better; right is always better.

  • Clean, calm, private environment

    Fresh linens every session, temperature-controlled rooms, and genuine quiet. The space is designed for recovery.
