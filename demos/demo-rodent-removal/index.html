The humane way — and the only way that works

  • No poison, ever

    Rodenticides create secondary poisoning risks for owls, hawks, and pets. We don't use them — period.

  • Exclusion first

    Trapping without sealing entry points is a revolving door. We seal first, trap second. That's why our results stick.

  • California-licensed wildlife handlers

    All technicians hold current CDFW permits for live wildlife handling and relocation.

  • Two-year exclusion warranty

    If any animal re-enters through our sealed points within two years, we fix it free.
