What we offer

From full breed cuts to a quick tidy-up — every service is done with care, quality shampoo, and a lot of patience.

Full Groom

Bath, blow-dry, brush-out, breed cut or custom trim, nail trim, ear cleaning, and bandana. The complete package.

Bath & Brush

Shampoo, condition, blow-dry, full brush-out, and a spritz of something nice. Great for maintenance between full grooms.

Nail Trim & Ear Clean

Quick, careful nail trim and gentle ear cleaning. Available as a standalone or add-on.

We take it easy

  • Fear Free certified groomers

    We use techniques that reduce anxiety and build trust — especially important for nervous or first-time dogs.

  • One dog at a time

    We don't run a kennel. Your dog is the focus of our attention for their entire appointment — no crate waiting.

  • Premium, pet-safe products

    We use Chris Christensen and Espree — professional grooming shampoos without harsh sulfates or artificial fragrance.

  • We'll tell you what we notice

    If we see a lump, a rash, or an ear issue — we'll let you know. Think of us as a second set of eyes for your dog's health.
