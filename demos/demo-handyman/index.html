Home Repairs

How it works

  • Tell Frank your list

    Email or call with everything you need done. He'll estimate the time and confirm the visit — usually within 48 hours.

  • Flat hourly rate, no surprises

    One clear rate covers labor. You pay for any materials at cost — no markup, no guessing.

  • Fully insured and background-checked

    Frank is licensed, insured, and has passed a full background check. You're in good hands.

  • He won't leave until it's right

    Frank takes pride in the work. If something doesn't look right, he fixes it — no argument, no extra charge.
