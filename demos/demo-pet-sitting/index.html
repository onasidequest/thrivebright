What we offer

Every service happens in your home, on your pet's schedule. No kennels, no crates, no strangers — just familiar surroundings and reliable care.

In-Home Pet Sitting

Daily drop-in visits — feeding, fresh water, playtime, potty breaks, and a full report with photos sent to you every visit.

Dog Walking

30 or 60-minute walks with GPS tracking. Your dog gets exercise and enrichment — and you get a photo update from the trail.

Overnight Stays

We stay in your home overnight so your pets sleep in their own beds. Full care from dinner to morning walk.

How we work

  • Free meet and greet first

    Before your first booking, we come to your home to meet your pets, learn their routines, and answer every question.

  • Photos and updates every visit

    You'll get a photo update and a short report card after every drop-in or walk — so you always know they're doing well.

  • Fully insured and background-checked

    We carry pet sitter insurance and have passed a thorough background check. Your home and your pets are protected.

  • Pet first aid certified

    We're certified in pet CPR and first aid. In a true emergency, we know exactly what to do and we'll contact your vet immediately.
