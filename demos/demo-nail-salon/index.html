The Lacquer & Co. difference

  • Sanitation you can see

    Single-use files and buffers. Autoclave-sterilized tools. Fresh liners every client. We don't cut corners on hygiene.

  • Products that are actually safe

    Non-toxic, 10-free polishes. No MMA acrylics — ever. Your nail health matters more than a cheap set.

  • No rushing, no upselling

    We take our time with every client. You'll never feel pressured to add services you didn't ask for.

  • A space that feels good

    Clean, bright, good music, actual ventilation. A salon that doesn't give you a headache.
