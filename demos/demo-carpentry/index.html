How we work

  • Every piece starts with a conversation

    We listen first — dimensions, wood species, finish, function. Then we design.

  • Locally sourced Maine hardwood

    We prefer working with New England timber when available — it's beautiful and supports the region.

  • Traditional joinery, modern technique

    Mortise and tenon, dovetails, hand-cut details — built the way furniture should be built.

  • Transparent timelines and pricing

    No surprise costs. We quote clearly and deliver on schedule — always.
