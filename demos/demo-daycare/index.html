Our programs

Every stage of childhood is different. Our programs meet kids exactly where they are.

Infant Care

Ages 6 weeks – 12 months. Low ratios, consistent caregivers, and a calm environment tailored to your baby's individual rhythm.

Toddler Program

Ages 1–3. Language-rich, hands-on exploration that builds independence and social skills.

Pre-K

Ages 3–5. Kindergarten readiness through structured play, STEM activities, and creative expression.

A place they can't wait to come back to

  • Play-based, child-led learning

    We follow the child's curiosity. Play is how kids learn best — our curriculum is built around that truth.

  • Low ratios, consistent teachers

    Your child bonds with the same caregivers every day. Relationships matter as much as curriculum.

  • Daily photo updates

    Know what your child is up to throughout the day. Our app keeps you connected without being intrusive.

  • Wisconsin-licensed, CPR-certified staff

    Every teacher is licensed, background-checked, and trained in early childhood development.
