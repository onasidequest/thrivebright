How we work

  • Free design consultation

    We sit down, understand your goals, and give you honest feedback on scope, timeline, and budget before you commit to anything.

  • Fixed-price contracts

    We price the job before we start. No open-ended estimates, no surprise invoices — you know the number before any work begins.

  • We pull all permits

    Every remodel is properly permitted. We handle the city, the inspections, and the paperwork — so you don't have to.

  • One point of contact, start to finish

    You'll have a project manager's direct number. No chasing trades, no miscommunication — just clear updates.
