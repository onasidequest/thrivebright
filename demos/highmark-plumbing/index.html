Our Story

A Corner Spot.
A Community Ritual.

Maple & Mule opened in 2019 with a single grinder, a borrowed espresso machine, and a stubborn belief that Dalton deserved something a little more intentional than drive-through coffee.

We named ourselves after the two things we love most about the South: maple syrup and mule-headed determination. Every morning at 5:30 AM, we start the grinders. Every evening at 6 PM, we lock up — tired, happy, and smelling like espresso.

Our beans come from three farms we visit every year. Our milk comes from a creamery 40 miles north. Our pastries come from a woman named June who starts her day at 3 AM and always shows up with flour on her chin.

"We're not trying to be the biggest coffee shop.
We're trying to be your favorite one."

— Founders, Maple & Mule
