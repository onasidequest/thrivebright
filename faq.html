Simple. You fill out the form on our homepage with basic info about your business — name, type, services, and style preferences. From there:

  1. 1. We design a custom website mockup tailored to your brand
  2. 2. We send it to you within 48 hours — you see it before paying anything
  3. 3. If you love it, we finalize and launch. If not, you owe nothing.

It's truly free — no credit card, no obligation.

We move fast:

  • → 48 hours to deliver your custom mockup after you submit the form
  • → 48 hours to go fully live after you approve the mockup and pay

So from form submission to a live website, you're looking at roughly 4 days — assuming you approve promptly. Most traditional agencies take 4–8 weeks.
