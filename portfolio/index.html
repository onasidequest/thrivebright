About Me

I'm Bri, a marketing consultant based in the San Francisco Bay Area. Since 2015, I've helped businesses of all sizes build and grow their online presence through strategic marketing, modern web design, and compelling content.

My experience spans startups to established brands. I've built national marketing event programs that drove record customer growth, developed content strategies that increased engagement by 200%+, and designed websites that convert visitors into paying customers.

I work independently, which means you're always communicating directly with the person doing the work. No hand-offs, no junior team members, just me bringing 10+ years of experience to every project.

Industries I've worked in: Home services, SaaS startups, hospitality, e-commerce, health and wellness, real estate, food and beverage, and non-profits.
